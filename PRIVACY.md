# Privacy Policy for TeXBloX

**Last updated:** November 03, 2025

## Overview

TeXBloX is a Confluence macro that renders LaTeX mathematical formulas. This privacy policy explains our data practices for the TeXBloX application.

## Data Collection

**TeXBloX does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.**

Specifically:
- **No user accounts:** TeXBloX does not create or manage user accounts
- **No personal information:** We do not collect names, emails, or any identifying information
- **No usage tracking:** We do not track how you use TeXBloX
- **No external connections:** TeXBloX operates entirely within your Confluence environment
- **No cookies:** TeXBloX does not set or use cookies

## Formula Content

The LaTeX formulas you enter into TeXBloX are:
- Stored as part of your Confluence page content
- Processed locally within your Confluence instance
- Subject to your organization's existing Confluence data policies
- Not transmitted to external servers or services

## Third-Party Services

TeXBloX does not integrate with or send data to any third-party services. All formula rendering happens locally using bundled libraries.

## Changes to This Policy

Any changes to this privacy policy will be posted here. We will update the "Last updated" date when changes are made.
